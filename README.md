# Pete's Fix It Up Shop

Offline credit-report accuracy checker. Load a report, find date and balance contradictions, and draft dispute letters. Everything stays in the browser on this device. No uploads. No analytics.

## Start here

1. Open the app (the live link, or `index.html` in a browser). On a phone, use the browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
2. On the Intake tab, use **Open practice file** first. That file is made-up.
3. For a real report, tap **PDF or photo** and pick a PDF or a picture (JPG, PNG, or WEBP). A text PDF is read directly. A photo or a scanned PDF is read on this device. You can also paste the text or type the accounts.
4. Read the Findings tab. Only contradictions are kept. Weak points and an earlier delinquency date stay out of the letter.
5. On Letters, read the short note above the draft. It says why that coding argument is being made. The first letter is a Metro 2 rebuttal. The second letter, after a “verified” result, asks who was contacted, what was sent, and what came back. It does not repeat the first letter.
6. Check every date against the report before you mail anything. The drafts are not legal advice.

## What it reads

- PDF with a real text layer, using the bundled PDF reader.
- Scanned or image-only PDFs, by drawing each page and reading the picture on this device.
- Photos: `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, and `.webp`, plus GIF, BMP, and TIFF. A HEIC photo is used when the phone’s browser can open it. If it cannot, save a JPG.
- Pasted text, and the practice file.

The words never leave the device. The text reader (Tesseract) and its English data are part of this app. Handwriting, a blurry photo, or a page shot at a steep angle often will not read. When that happens the app says so and you can paste the text or type the accounts.

## Letters

Letters quote only breaks the report actually contains: the payment grid against the last-payment date or the date reported, a first-delinquency date that does not match the late history or is past seven years, an original creditor and a collector both showing a balance, two bureaus that do not match, a discharged debt still listed as owed, a payment history that skips a month, or a status that does not match the newest coded month.

The shop will not write that an accurate account is not yours, will not dispute true history just to seek deletion, and will not ask to move an earlier delinquency date later. That can restart the seven-year clock.

The framework is the same: a bureau dispute under 15 U.S.C. §1681i, a furnisher dispute under §1681s-2 and 12 C.F.R. §1022.43, a method-of-verification letter after a “verified” result when the same break remains, then the CFPB fact sheet. Confirm the dispute address printed on the report before you mail.

## Install as an app

1. Open the app in Chrome (Android / Windows) or Safari (iPhone).
2. Android/Windows: browser menu → Install app / Add to Home screen.
3. iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen.

Open it once while online so the screens and the on-device text reader are saved. After that it can open with no connection. A passphrase on the Shield tab encrypts the bay on this device. If you forget it, the bay cannot be opened.
